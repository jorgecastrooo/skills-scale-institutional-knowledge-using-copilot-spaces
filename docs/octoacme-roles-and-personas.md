# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Subject Matter Expert (SME)

### Role Summary
Subject Matter Experts provide deep technical or domain expertise to guide project decisions and ensure quality deliverables. They advise on best practices and serve as a critical resource for technical validation throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Provide deep technical or domain expertise across project phases
- Review deliverables for accuracy and alignment with best practices
- Advise on technical feasibility and solutions
- Identify and flag potential risks based on domain knowledge
- Mentor team members in specialized areas

### Goals
- Ensure technical quality and accuracy of deliverables
- Reduce rework through early expert review
- Build team capability through knowledge transfer
- Minimize domain-related risks and oversights

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works closely with**: Project Manager, Development Team, Quality Assurance Lead
- **Consulted during**: Planning and execution phases
- **Reviews**: Technical designs, implementation approaches, quality standards
- **Escalates to**: Project Manager when domain-specific risks or blockers are identified

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Domain-specific guidance and best practice documentation
- Ad-hoc consultations with development and QA teams

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder/Sponsor Representatives ensure business alignment and advocate for stakeholder interests throughout the project. They communicate requirements, approve key decisions, and serve as the bridge between business needs and project execution.

### Responsibilities
- Ensure business alignment with project goals and outcomes
- Communicate stakeholder needs, constraints, and priorities
- Provide approval authority on key project decisions and trade-offs
- Review milestones and deliverables for business value
- Escalate issues and risks to executive leadership when needed

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers maximum business value
- Maintain stakeholder satisfaction and engagement
- Enable timely decision-making through clear authority delegation
- Reduce rework by validating requirements early

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Partners with**: Project Manager, Product Managers
- **Reviews**: Milestones, deliverables, and project progress
- **Provides input on**: Priority trade-offs, scope decisions, and success criteria
- **Escalates to**: Executive leadership for critical issues or scope changes

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and steering committee meetings
- Approval gates and decision logs
- Executive-level status updates and business impact reports

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define and oversee testing strategies to ensure deliverables meet quality standards and acceptance criteria. They collaborate with development and product teams to embed quality throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define comprehensive testing strategies and quality plans
- Oversee quality assurance processes and test execution
- Ensure deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Identify and track quality issues and defects
- Participate in release planning and deployment validation

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, reliable deliverables
- Catch defects early to reduce production issues
- Enable confident releases through rigorous testing
- Continuously improve quality processes

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Collaborates with**: Development Team, Product Managers
- **Participates in**: Release planning, sprint planning, and retrospectives
- **Validates**: Acceptance criteria, test coverage, and quality gates
- **Reports to**: Project Manager on quality status and risks

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality metrics dashboards
- Defect reports and quality reviews
- Sprint planning and release coordination meetings

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design system architecture and guide technical decisions to ensure projects are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards. They provide strategic technical leadership throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Design overall system architecture and technical approach
- Ensure technical feasibility of project requirements
- Provide guidance on technology choices and trade-offs
- Review technical designs and implementation approaches
- Advise on scalability, performance, and maintainability concerns

### Goals
- Ensure systems are scalable, maintainable, and performant
- Reduce technical debt and rework through sound architecture
- Align projects with organizational technical standards
- Enable team capability through technical mentoring

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with**: Development Team, Subject Matter Expert (SME)
- **Advises during**: Project planning and execution phases
- **Reviews**: Technical designs, architecture decisions, and implementation approaches
- **Escalates to**: Project Manager for architecture-related risks or constraints

### Typical Communication
- Architecture design documents and technical specifications
- Design reviews and technical deep-dives
- Technology selection and feasibility assessments

---

## Communications/Documentation Lead

### Role Summary
Communications and Documentation Leads manage internal and external communications, maintain project documentation, and ensure effective knowledge transfer. They enable alignment and transparency across stakeholders and preserve institutional knowledge.

### Responsibilities
- Manage internal and external project communications
- Create and maintain comprehensive project documentation
- Ensure knowledge transfer and documentation accessibility
- Coordinate communication across teams and stakeholders
- Maintain lessons learned and best practice repositories

### Goals
- Ensure clear, consistent communication across stakeholders
- Preserve institutional knowledge through documentation
- Enable team onboarding and knowledge sharing
- Reduce miscommunication and rework through clarity

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Partners with**: Project Manager and all team members
- **Plays key role during**: Release, deployment, and retrospective phases
- **Coordinates with**: Development Team, Product Managers, and Stakeholder Representatives
- **Supports**: Knowledge transfer and stakeholder engagement

### Typical Communication
- Release notes and deployment communications
- Project documentation and knowledge base articles
- Stakeholder updates and team announcements

---

## Risk Owner

### Role Summary
Risk Owners identify, assess, and monitor project risks, proposing mitigation strategies and escalating critical issues. They help the project team proactively manage uncertainties to enable successful delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and assess project risks across all phases
- Maintain and update risk registers
- Propose and track risk mitigation strategies
- Monitor risk indicators and escalate critical risks
- Conduct risk reviews with project stakeholders

### Goals
- Minimize project impact from identified risks
- Enable proactive risk management rather than reactive crisis response
- Ensure risk visibility and informed decision-making
- Reduce project delays and budget overruns caused by unmanaged risks

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with**: Project Manager and stakeholders
- **Actively involved in**: Risk identification, assessment, and communication phases
- **Reports to**: Project Manager on risk status and mitigation progress
- **Escalates to**: Stakeholder Representatives and executive leadership for critical risks

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and risk assessment reports
- Risk review meetings and escalation communications
- Mitigation strategy updates and effectiveness tracking
